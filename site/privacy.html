The short version

Privacy,
without the fog.

Auto YT Uploader is a personal Android app. It does one focused job: upload videos from folders you choose to your own YouTube channel as private videos, then move completed files into an album called Uploaded on your phone.

What the app accesses

  • Videos on your phone: the app reads the videos in the folders you choose, and, unless you turn this off, moves them to an album called Uploaded after they upload. The app also reads the names of your video folders so you can choose from them.
  • Your Google account: with your permission, the app uses the youtube.upload scope to upload videos to the YouTube channel of the Google account you sign in with. Google handles the sign-in process and the permissions it displays, and the app uses this permission only for its upload function.

How your data is used and shared

Videos, their filenames (used as titles) and upload requests are sent directly from your phone to YouTube. They are not sent to the developer or to anyone else. Videos are uploaded as private videos, and the app does not publish them publicly or select a different channel for you. The app has no server, no analytics and no advertising. The app does not sell your data.

What is stored

The app keeps your settings and a list of uploaded videos on your phone only, so that it does not upload the same video twice. Google Play services manages your sign-in on the phone.

Deleting your data and removing access

Uninstalling the app deletes its settings and upload list. You can remove the app's access to your account at any time at Google Account permissions. Delete uploaded videos in YouTube Studio.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google handles uploaded videos under the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
