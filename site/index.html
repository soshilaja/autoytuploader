a crowded camera roll.

  2. 02↑

The boundaries matter

A direct line.
Nothing extra.

Auto YT Uploader is built for one personal job: moving your videos to your own YouTube channel. It does not need a backstage pass to the rest of your account.

Your channel only

Permission for uploading

Google sign-in uses the YouTube upload permission only for the videos you choose to upload.

Each uploaded video is set to private, so it stays for you unless you decide otherwise.
